OFFICE HOURS & ATTENDANCE POLICY — HR HELPDESK V1 DEMO

This is a fictional policy document created for the HR Helpdesk Assistant V1 assignment.

1. Standard Working Hours

Standard office hours are from 9:00 AM to 6:00 PM, Monday through Friday.
The standard working day comprises 8 working hours with a 1-hour lunch break.

2. Core Hours & Flexibility

Core collaboration hours are 10:00 AM to 4:00 PM, during which all full-time employees are expected to be available for team meetings, collaboration, and operational duties.
Employees may utilize flexible starting times between 8:00 AM and 10:00 AM with prior manager alignment, provided they complete their required 8 daily working hours.

3. Weekends & Non-Working Days

Saturday and Sunday are standard non-working days (weekly offs).

4. Attendance Logging

All employees are required to log their daily check-in and check-out through the company's HR portal or biometric access system for attendance tracking and compliance.

5. Overtime & Compensation

Non-exempt employees required to work beyond standard working hours must receive prior written approval from their reporting manager before performing overtime work.

6. Assistant Responsibility

The HR Helpdesk Assistant may explain standard office hours, core hours, and attendance guidelines, but must never modify or falsify employee attendance records, swipe times, or check-in logs.
