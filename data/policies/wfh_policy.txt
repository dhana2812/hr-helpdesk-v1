WFH POLICY — HR HELPDESK V1 DEMO

This is a fictional policy document created for the HR Helpdesk Assistant V1 assignment.

1. Regular Work From Home

Employees may work remotely for a maximum of two days per week.

2. Friday Restriction

Friday is not eligible for regular work-from-home requests.

3. Probationer Eligibility

Employees currently on probation are not eligible to apply for regular work-from-home or remote work until they have successfully completed their standard 90-day (3 months) probation period.

4. Exceptions

Exceptions to the regular work-from-home policy require manager approval.

5. Assistant Responsibility

The HR Helpdesk Assistant may explain this policy but must not approve or reject a work-from-home request.

This policy is fictional and exists only for the HR Helpdesk Assistant V1 demonstration.
