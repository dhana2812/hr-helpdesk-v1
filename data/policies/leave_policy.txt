This is a fictional policy document created for the HR Helpdesk Assistant V1 assignment.

1. Casual Leave

Employees may use casual leave for short personal requirements.

Employees should submit a leave request through the company's approved leave process.

2. Sick Leave

Sick leave may be used when an employee is unable to work because of illness.

For extended sick leave, supporting medical documentation may be required according to company procedures.

3. Approval

The HR Helpdesk Assistant may explain the leave policy but must never automatically approve or reject employee leave.

Leave approval requires the appropriate authorized human manager.

4. Important

Employee leave balances must be retrieved from the deterministic employee records backend.

The assistant must never invent or estimate an employee's leave balance.
