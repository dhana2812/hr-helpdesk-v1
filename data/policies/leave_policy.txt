LEAVE POLICY — HR HELPDESK V1 DEMO

This is a fictional policy document created for the HR Helpdesk Assistant V1 assignment.

1. Casual Leave

Employees may use casual leave for short personal requirements.
Casual leave requests should be submitted at least 2 working days in advance through the company's approved HR portal, except in unforeseen emergencies.
Employees may carry forward a maximum of 8 unused casual leaves into the next calendar year. Any unused leaves exceeding 8 will lapse automatically.

2. Sick Leave

Sick leave may be used when an employee is unable to work because of illness or medical emergencies.
Employees should notify their manager on or before the start of the working day.
Employees may take emergency sick leave for 1 or 2 days without a doctor's certificate. For sick leave extending beyond 2 consecutive working days, supporting documentation from a registered medical practitioner is mandatory upon return to work.

3. Anti-Sandwich Leave Rule

Under Acme Corp's anti-sandwich leave rule, if an employee takes casual leave on working days immediately preceding and immediately following a weekend or company holiday (for example, taking casual leave on Thursday and the following Monday when Friday is an official company holiday), all intervening holidays and weekend days are counted as leave and deducted from the employee's casual leave balance.
In this scenario, a total of 4 days are deducted (Thursday, Friday holiday, Saturday/Sunday, and Monday), rather than just 2 days.

4. Leave Balance Exhaustion & Loss of Pay

If an employee's leave balance is exhausted (0 remaining days), no paid casual leaves can be granted.
Employees with exhausted leave balances who require time off must apply for Unpaid Leave / Loss of Pay (LOP), which requires prior written approval from their reporting manager and HR.

5. Maternity Leave

Eligible female employees are entitled to up to 26 weeks of fully paid maternity leave.
Employees must submit a formal leave application accompanied by a registered medical practitioner's certificate at least 8 weeks (2 months) prior to the expected date of delivery.
An extension of up to 4 additional weeks may be requested with medical justification, subject to HR and management approval.

6. Paternity Leave

Eligible male employees are entitled to 2 weeks (10 working days) of paid paternity leave for the birth or legal adoption of a child.
Paternity leave must be requested at least 2 weeks in advance and should be utilized within 6 months following childbirth or adoption date.

7. Leave Approval

The HR Helpdesk Assistant may explain leave policies, entitlements, and notice requirements, but must never automatically approve or reject employee leave.
All leave approvals require authorized human manager sign-off.

8. Important

Employee leave balances (such as available casual or sick leave days) must be retrieved from the deterministic employee records backend.
The assistant must never invent, estimate, or alter an employee's leave balance.
