REIMBURSEMENT POLICY — HR HELPDESK V1 DEMO

This is a fictional policy document created for the HR Helpdesk Assistant V1 assignment.

1. Submission & Timelines

Employees should submit reimbursement claims with all required itemized receipts and supporting documents through the approved company reimbursement portal.
All reimbursement claims must be submitted within 30 days of the expense date. Claims submitted after 30 days will be rejected.

2. Daily Meal & Late-Night Dinner Limits

Dinner expenses incurred while working late are strictly capped at $30 per day.
Only the amount up to $30 is reimbursable. Any amount spent beyond this cap (for example, if an employee spends $55 on dinner, only $30 is reimbursed) must be paid out-of-pocket by the employee ($25 out-of-pocket).

3. Explicit Non-Reimbursable Items & Alcohol Prohibition

Alcoholic beverages, personal entertainment items, tips exceeding 20%, and traffic violations are strictly non-reimbursable under company policy, even when entertaining clients at dinner or during business travel.

4. Processing & Approvals

Reimbursement claims are reviewed and processed by the finance and HR processing team.

5. Pending Claims

If a reimbursement claim remains pending beyond the normal processing period, the employee should contact the appropriate finance or HR support team for escalation.

6. Employee Status Lookup

The assistant may retrieve an authenticated employee's reimbursement status from the deterministic employee records backend when authorized.

7. Assistant Responsibility

The assistant must not modify reimbursement records or claim statuses.
