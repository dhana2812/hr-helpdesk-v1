This is a fictional policy document created for the HR Helpdesk Assistant V1 assignment.

1. Submission

Employees should submit reimbursement claims with the required supporting documents through the approved reimbursement process.

2. Processing

Reimbursement claims are reviewed by the appropriate finance or HR processing team.

3. Pending Claims

If a reimbursement remains pending beyond the normal processing period, the employee should contact the appropriate HR or finance support team.

4. Employee Status

The assistant may retrieve an employee's reimbursement status from the deterministic employee records backend when the user is authorized to access their own record.

5. Assistant Responsibility

The assistant must not modify reimbursement records or claim statuses.
