EMPLOYEE JOINING & ONBOARDING POLICY — HR HELPDESK V1 DEMO

This is a fictional policy document created for the HR Helpdesk Assistant V1 assignment.

1. Onboarding Documentation

All new employees must submit required onboarding documents on or before Day 1, including:
- Government-issued photo identification proof
- Educational degrees and professional certifications
- Relieving / experience letters from previous employers
- Tax forms and direct deposit banking details

2. Induction & Compliance Training

All new joiners must complete the mandatory company orientation, security compliance training, and Code of Conduct certification within the first 14 days of joining.

3. Provisioning of IT Assets & Access

Laptops, system credentials, corporate email accounts, and building access cards are provisioned by the IT & Facilities team on the employee's official date of joining upon HR verification.

4. Probation Period

The standard probation period for new employees is 3 months (90 days) from their date of joining.
Confirmation of employment is subject to a satisfactory performance assessment conducted by the reporting manager at the conclusion of the probation period.

5. Assistant Responsibility

The HR Helpdesk Assistant may guide new hires on onboarding processes, required documentation, and orientation schedules, but must never generate offer letters, modify onboarding records, or alter candidate profiles.
